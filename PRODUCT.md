# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

android

## Users

Viewers using an Android TV device from several metres away with a D-pad remote. They need to connect their own lawful Xtream-compatible service or local M3U playlist, browse live TV, films and series, and start playback without reaching for a phone.

## Product Purpose

Streamia TV provides a fast, TV-first media browser for Xtream-compatible accounts and extended M3U imports. Success means reaching live TV, a film or an episode in a few remote presses while preserving predictable navigation and offline catalogue access.

## Brand Personality

Premium and immersive: iOS/visionOS-inspired frosted glass over a dark, softly coloured backdrop, with a single vivid accent (#FF375F) reserved for primary actions and live/selected state. Very rounded corners throughout. The glass recedes behind the programme during playback while remaining unmistakably legible — and its focus target unmistakably located — from across the room.

## Anti-references

Do not imitate Netflix or another streaming brand. Avoid neon IPTV dashboards, crowded technical tables, decorative gradients used as noise rather than structure, tiny text, and — regardless of visual style — focus states that would be missed if the viewer could not perceive colour: focus must always also read through scale, a distinct glow/shadow, and a wide border, never colour alone.

## Design Principles

1. The remote is the primary input: every action works with D-pad, Select, Back, Menu, and media keys.
2. One screen, one task: browsing is structured; playback is immersive.
3. Focus is never ambiguous: the selected target is visible from across the room.
4. Large catalogues remain fast through lazy rendering, local grouping, caching, and stable keys.
5. Failures explain what the viewer can do next without exposing credentials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use large type, strong contrast, 48 dp minimum targets, clear non-colour focus treatment, content descriptions, predictable Back behaviour, and reduced state-only motion. The complete experience must be operable without touch or pointer input.
